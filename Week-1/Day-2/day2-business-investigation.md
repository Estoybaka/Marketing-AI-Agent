## 14. Agent Scope

### Lead Capture --- TARGET

-   Receive normalized inbound messages.
-   Detect potential lead intent.
-   Extract only information supplied by the customer.
-   Preserve source/channel.
-   Create or update lead record.
-   Pass the lead to qualification.

Must not diagnose, invent information, invent prices, or own calendar
scheduling.

### Lead Qualification --- TARGET

-   Read captured lead.
-   Identify missing information.
-   Apply approved qualification rules.
-   Route the lead.

**Critical:** actual qualification criteria are UNKNOWN and must not be
invented.

### Content --- TARGET

-   Receive approved source brief.
-   Produce platform-specific drafts.
-   Keep claims consistent with approved information.

Must not invent medical claims/prices or publish autonomously without
authorization.

### Sales Follow-up --- TARGET

-   Continue appropriate prospect conversations.
-   Answer approved informational questions.
-   Follow approved follow-up rules.
-   Detect consultation intent.
-   Hand consultation requests to Scheduling.

Must not diagnose, invent prices, or own calendar logic.

## 17. Day 2 Conclusion

Public information supports a clear customer-facing journey around home
healthcare, free assessment/consultation, personalized care planning,
care delivery, and ongoing family updates.

It does **not** establish the internal CRM, qualification rules, sales
stages, follow-up process, technical integrations, or exact human
handoffs.

Therefore, the Day 2 design should use those areas as **UNKNOWN**, while
defining the four agents at responsibility level only.
